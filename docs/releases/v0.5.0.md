Stemma 0.5.0 takes its look from [stemma-math/design](https://github.com/stemma-math/design): the site, the read-back page and the command line now share one design system.

## What upgrading means for a library

- **A new Lean dependency.** The site needs `StemmaDesign`, which comes with stemma's Lean package; `stemma upgrade` updates the dependencies (`lake update`), and the library's manifest gains it.
- **The site looks different.** Pages use Stemma's faces, colours and components, in light and dark. Central environments are highlighted, and signature states are coloured chips.
- No change to `stemma.toml`, signatures or the workflow.

## New

- The read-back page drawn with the design system, with an appearance switch, filter chips and toasts.
- The command line's colours, symbols and help in the design system's roles. `!` is now `▲`, and with `TERM=dumb` the symbols fall back to ASCII. `NO_COLOR` and output that is not a terminal stay plain.
